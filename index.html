Our Approach

More than a wedding. A feeling you'll remember forever.

Your wedding should never feel like a copy of someone else's dream. It should reflect your story, your values, and all the little things that make your relationship yours.

At Maison Amora, we bring thoughtful planning, considered design, and a calm, personal approach to every celebration. We take care of the moving pieces so you can be present for the moments that matter most.

Services

Every detail, thoughtfully considered.

  1. i.

    Full-Service Wedding Planning

    From the first conversation to the final farewell, we oversee your celebration with a considered plan, trusted coordination, and attention to every detail.

    For couples who want the complete experience.

  2. ii.

    Wedding Design & Styling

    We translate your vision into a cohesive visual world through color, florals, lighting, textures, stationery, and beautifully considered details.

    For celebrations with a distinct point of view.

  3. iii.

    Wedding Day Coordination

    We bring every element together behind the scenes, managing timelines, suppliers, and logistics so you can experience your day without the pressure.

    For couples who want to be fully present.

  4. iv.

    Destination Weddings

    From discovering the right setting to coordinating the guest experience, we help transform a beautiful destination into a celebration that feels entirely your own.

    For love stories that take you somewhere extraordinary.
